Scope: home, topic pages, audience hubs, guide articles, FAQ, 404. Visitor mode: Read.
Audience and job: a newcomer to Hamkor Bank on a phone finds the next step of their first days and reads it comfortably.
Owner verdicts: rejected A/B/C themes and the Girih direction (no ornament; too many bright colours). Pinned: the official logo and bank name lead; calm, premium, readable; one type family; restrained palette; topic animations; "Назад" everywhere. ## Direction contract
THESIS: The bank's own calm welcome: its logo and name on the deep brand green, then a quiet paper guide where every topic has its own small moving picture. Refuses ornament, per-section colours and card-with-shadow templates.
OWN-WORLD: Paper #F5F4F0 and near-black ink; the bank's deep green #0A2E2A for the first screen, footer, dock and curtain; logo green #1AA752 as the only accent. Manrope throughout; pills with round arrows; 20px cards without shadows; thin rules.
STORY: The newcomer sees the bank, chooses local or expat or a topic, reads calm pages with a clear way back and forward.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home on a phone: deep green field, the logo draws itself, "Hamkor Bank" large, the welcome line, two pill buttons above the dock. Desktop: the same text left, the large mark right.
FORM: canon executed at full fidelity (owner-pinned); seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: topic illustrations play with the scroll; page change is the bank's curtain with its mark.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
