You can bring your own car, but it is expensive and involves paperwork. Below are the main rules for temporary import.

## Fee-free period

- **Up to 90 calendar days** in one calendar year the car may stay in the country without a fee.
- **Condition:** the car is registered in a foreign country in the name of a non-resident.
- **Personal use only.** You may not work as a taxi, rent the car out or let people drive it who are not named in the import documents.

## Extension and fee

If the car stays in the country longer than 90 days, you have to pay a fee. Since May 2024 the amount does not depend on engine power and is fixed per day.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| BCA (since 1 September 2026) | 440,000 soums |
| Fee | 1 BCA for each day beyond 90 |
| Example: 30 days | 13.2 million soums |

> **Check with HR.** According to our information, an extension is arranged at a customs post before the 90 days run out, but we could not confirm the exact procedure. Check it on customs.uz or with your onboarding specialist in advance.

## Documents for the border

1. **Vehicle registration certificate** issued abroad.
2. The owner's **international passport**.
3. **Driving licence** — an international one, or a national one with a translation if required.
4. **Re-export undertaking.** A customs officer issues it at the border. Keep it until you leave the country.
5. **Insurance (compulsory motor liability, OSAGO).** According to our information, it is bought at the border crossing right after customs.

> **Check with HR.** We could not confirm how OSAGO insurance is arranged at the border. Check with your onboarding specialist before the trip.

## Rules and risks

- **Condition of the car.** The car must leave the country in the same condition, apart from normal wear and tear.
- **Overstaying.** If you exceed the period and do not pay the fee, you face large fines, the car being towed to an impound lot and even confiscation by court order.
- **Permanent import (customs clearance).** If you decide to keep the car for good, the benefits for small-engine cars were cancelled on 1 January 2026: the duty is 15% of the value plus $0.40 for each cm³ of engine capacity. This rule applies to permanent import, not temporary import.

> **Tip.** It is often simpler and cheaper to rent a car: according to the adaptation team, it costs about $300–500. This way you avoid fees, paperwork and waiting at the border.
