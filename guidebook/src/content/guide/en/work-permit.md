The bank's HR team handles this process. Below is a short overview of what happens and what you will need to do.

## Work permit

A foreign citizen can work in Uzbekistan once two documents are in place:

1. **The employer's permit** to hire foreign workers. The bank obtains it.
2. **Confirmation of your right** to work in Uzbekistan.

The procedure is set by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 244 of 25.03.2019. There is a simplified procedure for qualified and highly qualified specialists (Presidential Resolution PP-4008 of 07.11.2018).

> **Check with HR.** Ask the bank's HR team which procedure applies to you, which documents you need, and how long it takes to issue and extend the permit. Prepare your documents in advance and reply to HR requests without delay.

## PINFL

PINFL (ПИНФЛ) is the personal identification number of an individual. Without it you cannot use many services.

### Where to get it

1. Go to a **Public Services Centre** or the **migration office**.
2. Take your international passport.
3. You need to go in person, but a representative with a power of attorney can also apply for you.

### Why you need it

- logging in to **OneID**, the single sign-in system for government services;
- getting an **e-signature** (ЭЦП, electronic digital signature);
- tax services;
- banking apps and services.

> **Tip.** Get your PINFL in your first weeks after arrival. You will need it for almost every online service.
