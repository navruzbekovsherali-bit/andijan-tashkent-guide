Bukhara is called the "Dome of Islam". There are no skyscrapers in the centre — only sand-coloured walls hundreds of years old. There is no need to rush from one sight to the next here: it is better just to wander.

## How to get there

1. **The Afrosiyob train** — the best option. About **4 h 10 min** from Tashkent and about **1.5 h** from Samarkand.
2. **By plane** — Bukhara has an international airport. Flights from Tashkent run almost every day and take about an hour.
3. **Night train** — a compartment or sleeper, if you fancy the romance of the railway.

Buy train tickets on the official website [eticket.railway.uz](https://eticket.railway.uz) — they sell out fast.

## What to see

The whole old town of Bukhara is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

### The Po-i-Kalyan ensemble

The city's calling card. Legend has it that the Kalyan minaret so impressed Genghis Khan that he ordered it to be spared. Step into the mosque's inner courtyard — it is completely silent.

### The Ark fortress

The ancient citadel of the emirs of Bukhara — a "city within a city" on a hill. There are museums inside.

### Lyabi-Hauz

A square with a pond (hauz) surrounded by old mulberry trees. Sit in a café by the water, order tea with parvarda sweets and watch city life go by.

### Chor-Minor

A small mosque with four little towers, hidden among residential streets. A very photogenic and cosy place.

## What to buy

- **Trading domes (toki).** Under the vaults of Toki Zargaron and Toki Sarrafon, just as hundreds of years ago, people sell metalwork, knives, silk and jewellery.
- **Blacksmiths' workshops.** The local stork-shaped knives and scissors are fine craftsmanship.
- **Gold embroidery.** A purse or waistcoat hand-embroidered with gold thread makes a great souvenir.

## What to try

1. **Bukhara plov (osh-i-sofi).** It is cooked in layers in copper cauldrons and is lighter than the Tashkent kind. Look for it in oshkhonas (plov canteens).
2. **Alat samsa** — thin pastry and plenty of juicy meat.
3. **Silk Road Spices tea house** — a tasting of tea with spices and saffron.

> **Tip.** [2GIS](https://2gis.uz) makes it easy to find your way through the narrow lanes of the old town.

## The perfect evening

Wait for sunset on Po-i-Kalyan Square, when the yellow brick walls turn orange. Then have dinner at a restaurant with a rooftop terrace and look at the city from above.
