Your key mission in our team is to strengthen the bank's expertise and develop its people. We believe your experience will be a catalyst for big changes.

## Main tasks

1. **Sharing knowledge and best practices.** Bring international experience and modern methods into everyday processes.
2. **Preparing local successors.** Train your colleagues and be a mentor, so the bank can grow steadily in the long term.
3. **Taking part in the bank's transformation.** Contribute to innovation and to improving the business model.
4. **High quality standards.** Lead by example in professionalism and follow global performance standards.

## The formula for success

Your success is measured not only by your personal achievements but also by your team's growth.

> **Important.** The handover of key expertise and the preparation of a successor must be completed before your contract ends.
