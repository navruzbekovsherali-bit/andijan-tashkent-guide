Tashkent is the largest city in Central Asia. Here ancient trade routes meet modern business centres, and the city stays calm and safe.

## About the city

Three eras live side by side in Tashkent:

- **The Old City:** mahallas, bazaars and mosques.
- **Soviet modernism:** wide avenues, monumental architecture and lots of greenery.
- **The new city:** modern housing complexes and business centres.

**Languages.** Uzbek is the official language, Russian is heard everywhere, and English is increasingly used in business and among young people.

## Climate

- **Summer** is very hot and dry, from +40 to +45 °C.
- **Winter** is mild, around 0 °C on average, but it can sometimes drop to −10 °C.
- **The best time** is April–May and September–October.

> **Tip.** In winter, during the heating season, keep an eye on air quality (AQI) in apps and choose hours with clean air for walks.

## Transport

1. **Taxis** are the most popular way to get around. Order through **Yandex Go** or **MyTaxi**. Check the fare in the app before you order.
2. **The metro** is one of the most beautiful in the world: every station has its own design. It covers the key districts and saves you from traffic jams.
3. **Buses:** a well-developed network, including electric buses.

## Why it is easy to live here

- **Digital services.** Many government services are available by phone, and you can pay in a café straight from a fintech app.
- **Hospitality.** People will help you find your way, and sometimes simply offer you a cup of tea.

## What to see first

- **Tashkent City Park:** a modern park in the centre with musical fountains and a planetarium.
- **Chorsu Bazaar:** to get a taste of the real Uzbekistan.
- **State Museum of Applied Arts:** for lovers of beauty and history.
