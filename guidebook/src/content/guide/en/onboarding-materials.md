In your first days, your onboarding specialist will send or show you a set of guides. They will help you quickly understand the main rules and work tools. All materials are available in Russian and Uzbek.

## What the set includes

1. **Onboarding dashboard** — an overall map of your adaptation: what you need to do and when.
2. **Health and safety** — the bank's basic safety rules.
3. **IceWarp corporate email** — how to sign in to your mailbox and start using it.
4. **IT Service Desk** — how to contact IT support if something does not work.
5. **EDM (electronic document management)** — how to write an internal memo and work with documents.

> **Tip.** Keep the guides somewhere handy: people often come back to them in the first weeks. If you are missing a guide, ask your onboarding specialist for it.
