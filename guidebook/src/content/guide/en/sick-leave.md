Life can be unpredictable: you may fall ill or need to deal with personal matters. Below is how to arrange everything properly, so you can relax and your work does not suffer.

## If you fall ill

Your health comes first. If you feel unwell, do not force yourself to come to work.

1. **Tell your manager and HR specialist straight away.** Write or call as early as possible, so colleagues have time to cover your tasks.
2. **See a doctor.** Only an official **certificate of incapacity for work** (sick note) justifies missing work and paying the benefit.
3. **No paper document is needed.** Since 01.01.2024 the sick note is issued only in **electronic form**, and asking for a paper one is not allowed. Let HR know when your sick leave is closed.

### Who pays and how much

From **01.07.2026** a new procedure applies:

- the employer pays the **first 5 days** of sick leave;
- **from day 6** the benefit is paid by the Social Insurance Fund, directly to your card through the **"Baraka"** system;
- to receive it, you need **at least 6 months** of service.

The benefit amount is set by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 314 of 31.05.2024:

| Length of service | Benefit |
|---|---|
| 6 months to 8 years | 60% of earnings |
| more than 8 years | 80% of earnings |

> **Check with HR.** As of October 2026 the procedure for payments through "Baraka" is still new, so check the details with HR. Some categories of employees receive a higher benefit (up to 100%). Ask HR whether you are entitled to it.

## If you need time off

1. **Agree the date** with your manager at least 1–2 days in advance.
2. **Submit a request** in the time-tracking system or write an application. This is needed for an accurate timesheet.
3. **Hand over urgent tasks** to colleagues or finish them before you leave.

### How time off works under the law

- **Unpaid leave** (Art. 241 of the Labour Code): at your request, up to 3 months in total per year.
- **Rest for working on a day off** (Art. 209 of the Labour Code): work on a day off is paid at double rate, or you are given another day off instead.

Your employer must consider your application. In some cases, except emergencies, they may refuse if your absence is critical for the work.

## Time tracking

Record when you arrive and leave in the electronic time-tracking system. Accurate data helps avoid mistakes in calculating your salary and holiday pay, and makes sure every hour you work is counted.

## Who to contact

- **Sick leave and time off:** your manager or HR manager.
- **Technical questions about time tracking:** the system administrator or the IT department.
- **Employment law questions:** the HR department.

Articles refer to the new Labour Code (in force since 30.04.2023).
