The mountains near Tashkent — Chimgan, Amirsoy, Beldersay — are good at any time of year. In winter people ski here, in spring the tulips bloom, in summer people escape the heat (about +25 °C when the city is +45 °C), and in autumn the forests turn crimson.

## How to get there

The mountains are 65–90 km from Tashkent.

1. **Car or taxi** — the most convenient way. Type "Amirsoy" or "Chimgan" into Yandex Go. The journey takes about **1.5 hours**.
2. **Suburban train + taxi.** Take a suburban train from the North or South Station to **Khojikent** station (about 1.5 h), then a local taxi to the resort area — 20–30 minutes.
3. **Minibus.** From Buyuk Ipak Yuli metro station to Gazalkent, then change to a taxi.

## What to see

### Amirsoy Resort

The most modern resort in Uzbekistan.

- **What to do:** ride the gondola up to **2,290 metres**. The views are worth the trip even if you do not ski.
- **In summer:** hiking trails and quad biking.
- **Website:** [amirsoy.com](https://amirsoy.com)

### Chimgan

A legend of mountaineering: simpler than Amirsoy, but with more soul.

- **What to do:** ride the old open chairlift, go horse riding in the foothills or hike up to the waterfalls.

### Charvak Reservoir

A huge turquoise "sea" ringed by mountains.

- **What to do:** go paragliding with an instructor (take-off from Mount Kumbel).
- **Where to relax:** there are many leisure areas along the shore, such as Avenue Park or Piramidy.

## Where to stay and eat

- **Layner Hotel** in Chimgan — for a family holiday: large grounds, swimming pools, children's entertainment and good food.
- **Chaykhana 2290 restaurant** — at the top of Amirsoy. Hot tea with a view of snow-covered ridges.
- **"Bochki" (Khumsan/Yusupkhana)** — a stop on the way to the mountains. People try pickled onions, mountain honey and fresh fish here.

## Useful tips

1. **Weather.** It is 10–15 °C colder in the mountains than in Tashkent. Even in summer, bring a windbreaker or a hoodie.
2. **Queues.** At weekends, arrive at Amirsoy at opening time so you do not have to queue for the cable car.
3. **Signal.** There is mobile signal almost everywhere, but it can drop in deep gorges. Download offline maps in advance.

## A plan for a day off

Breakfast in Tashkent in the morning, coffee at the top of Amirsoy by 11:00, Charvak after lunch, shashlik in a mountain teahouse in the evening.
