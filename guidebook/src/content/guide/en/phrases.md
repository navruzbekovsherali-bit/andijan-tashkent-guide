In Uzbekistan you will be understood in Russian and in Uzbek, and English is spoken in many offices and hotels. But a couple of phrases in the other person's language is the best way to earn a smile — and the juiciest melon at the bazaar.

In offices and business centres people mostly speak Russian; at markets they mostly speak Uzbek.

> **How to read.** The official Uzbek alphabet is Latin, but Cyrillic is still used on signs and in everyday life, so both are given below. The letter **q / қ** is a deep "k" from the back of the throat, **h / ҳ** is a soft "h", **o‘ / ў** is something between "o" and "u", and **x / х** is like the "ch" in Scottish "loch".

## Greetings

| Latin | Cyrillic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Assalomu alaykum | Ассалому алайкум | Hello (polite) |
| Salom | Салом | Hi |
| Xayrli tong / kun / kech | Хайрли тонг / кун / кеч | Good morning / afternoon / evening |
| Rahmat! | Раҳмат! | Thank you |
| Iltimos | Илтимос | Please |
| Xayr! | Хайр! | Goodbye |

> **Tip.** When you thank or greet someone, put your right hand on your heart — it is a sign of sincerity and respect.

## At the market and in shops

| Latin | Cyrillic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Qancha turadi? | Қанча туради? | How much is it? |
| Men buni olaman | Мен буни оламан | I will take this |
| Sizda ... bormi? | Сизда ... борми? | Do you have...? |
| Kassa qayerda? | Касса қаерда? | Where is the checkout? |

> **Tip.** Once you have agreed on a price, tell the seller "Baraka toping" — "may you be blessed". It is a very kind wish.

## In a taxi

| Latin | Cyrillic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Meni bu manzilga olib boring | Мени бу манзилга олиб боринг | Take me to this address |
| Qancha bo‘ladi? | Қанча бўлади? | How much will it be? |
| Shu yerda to‘xtang | Шу ерда тўхтанг | Stop here |

When giving an address, just show the point on the map on your phone.

## In a restaurant

| Latin | Cyrillic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Menyu, iltimos | Меню, илтимос | The menu, please |
| Hisob, iltimos | Ҳисоб, илтимос | The bill, please |
| Suv bo‘ladimi? | Сув бўладими? | Could I have some water? |

## Just in case

| Latin | Cyrillic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Yordam bering! | Ёрдам беринг! | Help! |
| Shifokor chaqiring | Шифокор чақиринг | Call a doctor |
| Men tushunmayapman | Мен тушунмаяпман | I do not understand |
