This is the strictest rule for foreigners in Uzbekistan. Remember one number — **3 days**.

<div class="video-reel" data-reel="stay"></div>

> **Important.** **Within 3 days of arriving, and after every move, you must be registered at your new place of stay.** Miss it and you face a large fine, and if the fine is not paid — expulsion from the country (deportation).

## How it works

1. **You land and check into a hotel.** The hotel registers you within 3 days. Make sure it has, and ask for the slip when you check out.
2. **You move to another hotel.** You are registered again — the new hotel does it, also within 3 days.
3. **You move into a flat.** Agree with the landlord that they will register your temporary stay within 3 days — on [emehmon.uz](https://emehmon.uz) or at the migration office. You cannot do it yourself.
4. **Tell the adaptation specialist your new address** every time you move.

## An example

| When | What happened | Who registers you, and by when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 November | landed, hotel | the hotel — by 4 November |
| 10 November | moved to another hotel | the new hotel — by 13 November |
| 20 November | moved into a flat | the owner of the flat — by 23 November |

> **Check with HR.** Whether these are working or calendar days under the new registration law (ZRU-1074, in force since October 2025) has not been confirmed yet. To be safe, count calendar days.

## A flat: agree in advance

- **Before you sign the lease**, ask the owner whether they will register you and sign an official lease. If they refuse, look for another flat: without registration, the problems are yours.
- **Get the documents ready:** your passport, the lease and the slip from your previous place, if you have one.
- **Check the result:** ask the owner to show you confirmation that you are registered.
- **Not sure?** Tell the adaptation specialist straight away — they will help.

## Fines

| Late by | Fine |
|---|---|
| up to 10 days | 5 BCA — 2.2 million soums |
| 10 to 30 days | 10 BCA — 4.4 million soums |
| more than 30 days | 20 BCA — 8.8 million soums |

Amounts are based on a BCA of 440,000 soums (from 01.09.2026). Both you and your host can be fined. If the fine is not paid, you face expulsion and a 1-year entry ban, or 3 years for a repeat offence.

> **Tip.** Keep every registration slip and take a photo of each: you may need them when your documents are processed.
