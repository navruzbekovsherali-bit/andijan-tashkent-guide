Most services are linked to a local phone number. So the first thing to do is buy a SIM card from one of the operators: Beeline, Ucell, Mobiuz or Uztelecom.

## Taxis and navigation

- **Yandex Go:** the main taxi and delivery service in the country. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id472650686)
- **MyTaxi:** taxis, works only in Tashkent.
- **inDrive:** taxis where you suggest the fare yourself. Handy for trips between cities.
- **2GIS:** detailed offline maps with building entrances and bus routes. [2gis.uz](https://2gis.uz)

## Money and payments

Paying by QR code works almost everywhere here, from supermarkets to pharmacies.

- **Hamkorbank:** the bank's mobile app. [Official page](https://hamkorbank.uz/en/personal-banking/mobile-banking)
- **Click:** payments and transfers. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id768132591)
- **Payme:** payments and transfers. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1093525667)

## Food and grocery delivery

- **Uzum Tezkor:** fast delivery from restaurants and shops.
- **Express24:** one of the oldest delivery services, with a wide choice of local places. [express24.uz](https://express24.uz)
- **Korzinka:** grocery delivery from the largest supermarket chain. [korzinka.uz/app](https://korzinka.uz/app/)

## Government services

- **MyGov:** the single government services portal: PINFL, registration status, taxes, certificates. [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/uz/app/mygov-uz/id1544175166)
- **MyID:** biometric identification. You need it to sign up for banking apps and government services. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562842955)
- **E-mehmon:** registration at your place of stay. [emehmon.uz](https://emehmon.uz)

## Shopping, travel and messaging

- **Uzum Market:** the country's main online marketplace, delivery usually within 1 day. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1640483056)
- **Train tickets:** buy tickets for trains. [eticket.railway.uz](https://eticket.railway.uz)
- **Telegram:** in Uzbekistan this is more than a messenger. It is the main place for news, talking to companies and support bots.

On Android, search for these apps in Google Play by name.

## Tips

1. **Get your PINFL.** Without your personal number, banking apps do not work fully. PINFLs are issued at Public Services Centres. There is more on the "Work permit and PINFL" page.
2. **Register your IMEI.** If you use a local SIM card, register your phone on [uzimei.uz](https://uzimei.uz). Otherwise it will be blocked on local networks.
3. **Subscribe to useful Telegram bots** of the utility companies and the tax service. It is the easiest way to check balances and charges.

> **Check with HR.** Sources give different deadlines for IMEI registration: 30 or 60 days from when you first connect a local SIM card. Check the exact deadline on uzimei.uz and do not put it off.
