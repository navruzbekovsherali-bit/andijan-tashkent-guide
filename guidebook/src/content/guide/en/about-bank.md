Hamkorbank was founded in 1991. Today it is one of the largest banks in Uzbekistan, with a wide branch network and a high reliability rating.

## Structure and reliability

Hamkorbank is a bank with international participation.

- **Shareholders.** The key investors include the International Finance Corporation (IFC, World Bank Group) and the Netherlands Development Finance Company (FMO).
- **Network.** Branches and banking service centres operate throughout Uzbekistan. According to the latest official data (2022), there are 47 branches and 104 offices.
- **Ratings.** Moody's — **B1**, S&P — **BB-**, with a "stable" outlook from both.

> **Check with HR.** Check the current shareholder structure and the current number of branches and offices with the bank: the data above comes from the latest public sources.

## Transformation and your contribution

The bank is now going through a large-scale transformation. Together with international experts, we are:

- updating the business model;
- strengthening governance standards;
- building a culture that meets global requirements.

Our focus is quality, fast decisions and professional expertise. Your experience will help the bank become the best at this.

## Digital services

For most operations you do not need to visit a branch: the essentials are on your smartphone.

- **Official website** — [hamkorbank.uz](https://hamkorbank.uz): exchange rates and product information.
- **Mobile app** — bill payments, transfers and currency exchange around the clock. Links to the iOS and Android app are on the [mobile banking page](https://hamkorbank.uz/en/personal-banking/mobile-banking).
