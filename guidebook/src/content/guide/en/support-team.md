The first weeks in a new place always bring a flood of information. The main rule: **if you have a question or a doubt, ask straight away**. We are here to help you find your rhythm, not to judge every step.

## Your Buddy (mentor)

Your Buddy is a colleague who knows our processes well and is happy to share their experience. You can come to them with any question, even an everyday one.

- **How things work:** explains internal processes and helps you find your way through the regulations.
- **Team culture:** tells you about our values, traditions and how we usually communicate.
- **Everyday details:** where the coffee is, how the printer works and who to go to if your pass does not work.

## Your team

You will work with your team every day.

- **Growth:** you can always count on your colleagues' support and knowledge.
- **Atmosphere:** we build a friendly, open environment where everyone's opinion is valued.
- **Difficult tasks** we solve together.

## HR Business Partner (HRBP)

Your HR partner makes sure you are comfortable at work and that all formalities are done on time.

- **Documents:** paperwork, certificates and employment law questions.
- **Health:** healthcare and insurance.
- **Relocation:** if you have moved from another city, your HRBP will help with relocation and housing.

> **Tip.** Do not hesitate to message colleagues on Telegram: people here usually reply quickly and to the point. If you do not know who to ask, write to your Buddy. They will point you in the right direction.
