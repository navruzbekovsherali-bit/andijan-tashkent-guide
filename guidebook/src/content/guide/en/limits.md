Your limits depend on your role and authority. They apply to three types of expenses:

- **accommodation** — hotel bookings and lodging on business trips;
- **flights** — planning and booking air travel;
- **business travel expenses** — per diems and other expenses provided for by the bank's rules.

## Where to find your limits

The limits are set by the **bank's internal regulations** and the **terms of your offer**.

1. Look at the terms in your offer.
2. If you need exact amounts or an explanation, contact the bank's **accounting department** or **HR**. They will tell you which limits apply to you.

> **Tip.** Check your limits before you submit a business trip request: the amounts in the request must stay within them. To learn how to arrange a trip, see the "Business trips" page.
