Life in Uzbekistan costs less than in Europe or the USA. Below are price guides and the rules for reimbursing housing costs.

## Renting a home

Costs depend a lot on the city. Tashkent is a busy metropolis; Andijan is calmer and noticeably more affordable.

| Flat | Tashkent, centre | Andijan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 room | ≈ $450–800 | ≈ $200–400 |
| 2 rooms | ≈ $600–1200 | noticeably cheaper than Tashkent |
| 3 rooms | ≈ $800–1500 | noticeably cheaper than Tashkent |

Prices are per month, as of 2026.

> **Check with HR.** Rent prices are only a guide: they depend on the area, the building and the season, and they change quickly. By our information, utilities are about $70–100 a month in Tashkent and $50–70 in Andijan, but these figures are not confirmed, and heating tariffs in Tashkent may go up.

> **Tip.** When looking for a home, ask about the mahalla, the local neighbourhood community. Living in a good mahalla means being safe and close to neighbours who will always help.

## What the bank reimburses

### The bank pays

1. **Rent:** strictly within the limit stated in your individual offer.
2. **Estate agent's services:** once, in the amount stated in your offer.

### You pay yourself

**The deposit.** For long-term rentals, owners almost always ask for a deposit, usually equal to one month's rent.

> **Important.** The deposit is not included in the bank's limit. You pay it from your own money.

How the deposit is usually returned:

- in full when you move out, if everything in the flat is in order;
- sometimes, by agreement with the owner, it counts as payment for the last month.

> **Tip.** Before you sign the contract, make sure it clearly sets out how the deposit will be returned or offset.

The bank's HR team will help and recommend trusted estate agents.

## Food and restaurants

- **Groceries for a month:** about $150–250 in Tashkent, $100–200 in Andijan.
- **Lunch in a café:** $4–7.
- **Dinner in a good restaurant:** $10–20.
- **Coffee in a coffee shop:** $2–3.

> **Tip.** Visit a local bazaar: Chorsu in Tashkent or the central markets in the regions. The produce is fresh, and bargaining is part of the local culture.

## Transport

- **Taxi:** check the exact fare in the Yandex Go app or, in Tashkent, MyTaxi.
- **Metro (Tashkent only):** 2500 soums by card or app, 3000 soums in cash.
- **Petrol:** about $0.80–1.00 per litre.

There is more on the "Getting around the city" page.

## Monthly budget

Guide figures for one person, including housing:

- **Modest:** $800–1000.
- **Comfortable:** $1500–2500.
- **Premium:** from $3000.

## Checklist: choosing a flat

Before signing the contract, check:

1. **Air conditioning.** It is not a luxury: in summer it can be above +40 °C.
2. **Hot water.** Is there a water heater (boiler) in case of planned outages?
3. **Internet.** Is fibre-optic internet connected to the building?
4. **Location.** Are supermarkets (for example, Korzinka, Makro) and pharmacies nearby?
5. **Contract.** Are utilities and the rental tax included in the price?
