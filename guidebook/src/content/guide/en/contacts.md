Below is who to contact for each question.

## Who to ask about what

| Question | Who to contact |
|---|---|
| First days, onboarding, guides, "I don't know who to ask" | Onboarding specialist: Sherali |
| How processes work, everyday details, meeting the team | Your Buddy |
| Tasks, goals, KPIs, approving holidays and days off | Your manager |
| Healthcare and insurance, relocation and housing, employment law questions | HR Business Partner (HRBP) |
| Employment contract, HR documents, certificates of employment | Personnel Department |
| Computer, email, passwords, system access | IT Service Desk |
| Salary, tax certificate, business trips, advances and limits | Accounting |

## More detail

- **Onboarding specialist (Sherali):** your main contact in the first weeks. Helps with documents, your bank card and newcomer guides.
- **Buddy:** a colleague who acts as your mentor. They will show you where things are and how we usually work.
- **Your manager:** sets tasks, holds goal meetings and approves holidays and days off.
- **HR Business Partner:** makes sure you are comfortable at work and helps with health, insurance and relocation.
- **Personnel Department:** handles your hiring and HR documents.
- **IT Service Desk:** solves technical problems and login errors.
- **Accounting:** payments, tax certificates, business trips, advances and spending limits.

> **Tip.** We will add phone numbers and email addresses to this page later. For now, ask your onboarding specialist for the contact you need.
