A business trip has three steps: a request before the trip, the trip itself and a report after you return.

## 1. Before the trip: a request in EDM

Create a "Business trip" document in the EDM system and include:

- **Purpose** — why you are going (a meeting, a project, training).
- **Place** — the city and organisation you are going to.
- **Dates** — departure date, return date and number of days.
- **Expenses** — flights, accommodation and other costs. Attach a cost estimate with the amounts.

The amounts must stay within the bank's limits. To find out your limits, see the "Travel and expense limits" page.

The request must be **approved and signed by all responsible managers**. Only travel once the request has been signed.

**Need money before the trip?** You can get an advance. To arrange it, contact the accounting department.

## 2. During the trip

- You pay the expenses yourself; the bank reimburses them after checking.
- Choose accommodation within the bank's limit.
- **Keep the originals of all receipts**, tickets and boarding passes.

## 3. After you return: the report

**Submit your report within 3 days of returning.**

1. Prepare a **business trip report**: what was done, the results and the actual expenses.
2. Attach the original receipts and supporting documents.
3. Send the originals by post to the Head Office (Andijan).
4. The accounting department checks the documents. The bank reimburses the expenses together with your next salary.

> **Important.** A request signed before the trip and the original documents are the two conditions without which reimbursement is impossible.
