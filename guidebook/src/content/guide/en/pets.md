The import and export of pets is controlled by Uzbekistan's veterinary service.

> **Check with HR.** In May 2026 the State Veterinary Committee was reorganised. Check with your onboarding specialist which body is now responsible for importing animals.

## Documents and veterinary requirements

| What you need | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Microchip | Done **before** the rabies vaccination. The chip number is entered in the pet passport |
| International pet passport | Owner's details, chip number, all vaccinations |
| Rabies vaccination | No earlier than 12 months and no later than 30 days before the trip |
| Combined vaccination | Dogs: distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, adenovirus, leptospirosis. Cats: panleukopenia, calicivirus, rhinotracheitis |
| Parasite treatment | Against worms and fleas/ticks 24–48 hours before departure, recorded in the passport |
| Veterinary certificate (Form No. 1) | Issued by a state veterinary clinic 1–5 days before the trip. At the airport it is exchanged for an international veterinary certificate |

> **Check with HR.** The rabies vaccination timing and the Form No. 1 requirement come from travel websites — we did not find the official document. Check them with the veterinary service before the trip.

## Carrier requirements (IATA standard)

The requirements are based on the rules of the International Air Transport Association (IATA).

- **The carrier** is rigid (plastic or metal), with a secure lock.
- **Size:** the animal can stand up, turn around and lie down naturally.
- **Ventilation** on three sides.
- **The floor** is covered with absorbent material.
- **A water bowl** is fixed inside and can be filled without opening the carrier.

## Step by step

1. **Arrange transport with the airline** right after buying your ticket: the number of places for animals on board is limited. Uzbekistan Airways' rules are on [uzairways.com](https://www.uzairways.com).
2. **Check the requirements of the destination country** if you are taking a pet *out of* Uzbekistan. For example, the EU and the UAE may require a rabies antibody test.
3. **Get the veterinary certificate (Form No. 1)** at a state veterinary clinic 2–3 days before departure.
4. **Arrive at the airport 3–4 hours early.** First go through veterinary control in the terminal and get the international certificate, then go to check-in.

## Living with a pet in Uzbekistan

- **Number.** Under veterinary rules one person may bring no more than 2 dogs or cats.
- **Renting a home.** Many landlords, especially in new buildings, are wary of animals. Be sure to include your pet in the rental agreement to avoid eviction without your deposit back.
- **Registration.** If you live in the country for a long time, register your pet with the local veterinary service.

> **Check with HR.** We could not confirm whether importing up to 2 animals is exempt from customs duties. See the rules for personal belongings on [customs.uz](https://customs.uz).
