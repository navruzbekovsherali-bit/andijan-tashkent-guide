Moving and starting a new job is always exciting. This checklist will help you remember everything and feel confident from your first days in Uzbekistan.

## Documents and money

Put these in your hand luggage so they are always close at hand.

1. **International passport.** Check that it is valid for at least 6 more months on the day you enter the country.
2. **Cash (USD or EUR).** Bring new notes with no marks or tears. They are easier to exchange at a good rate.
3. **Bank cards.** Visa and Mastercard are accepted in large cities, but at markets and in small cafés you will need cash in soums.
4. **Prescriptions for your medicines.** If you are bringing strong medicines, take a prescription in English or Russian.

## First-aid kit

1. **Your usual medicines.** Since 15.12.2025 antibiotics in Uzbekistan are sold only with an electronic prescription. Bring a supply of what you normally use.
2. **Remedies for digestion.** Local food is delicious but rich. Digestive enzymes and sorbents will come in handy at first.
3. **Medicines with special status.** Codeine, phenazepam and similar medicines are sold only on prescription. To bring them in, you need a declaration and supporting documents.

> **Check with HR.** The rules for bringing in personal medicines are changing: the lists of controlled substances are updated from 12.01.2027. If you are bringing prescription medicines, ask your onboarding specialist or check customs.uz in advance to find out which documents you need.

## Clothes

1. **Layers.** The climate is sharply continental. Even after a hot day, evenings in the mountains or the desert can be cool.
2. **Clothes for special places.** For visiting mosques and walking in conservative areas, women should bring a headscarf and clothes that cover the shoulders and knees.
3. **Sun protection.** The sun is strong, so SPF 30+ sunscreen and sunglasses are a must.

## Customs rules

### Duty-free allowance

| How you enter | Duty-free limit |
|---|---|
| By air | up to $1000 |
| By train | up to $500 |
| By car or on foot | up to $300 |

To use the air allowance, you must have been abroad for at least 3 days. Jewellery counts towards this overall value limit.

### Cash

There is no limit on bringing in currency, but a large amount must be declared in writing.

- Above the equivalent of **100 million soums** (about $8,000): a written declaration is required.
- You may declare a smaller amount if you wish. When you leave, you can take out up to 100 million soums without a declaration; more only up to the amount you declared on entry.

Source: the customs rules explained on gov.uz. The rules change from time to time, so check customs.uz before you travel.

### Phone

Declare your phone when you enter — easiest in the "Yo‘lovchi deklaratsiyasi" app, then show the QR code at customs. Phones worth up to **$1,000 in total by air** (no more than two) are duty-free, up to $500 by train and up to $300 by car or on foot. Above the limit the duty is 30% of the excess. You cannot register the IMEI without a customs document. There is more on the "Your first 72 hours after arrival" page.

> **Tip.** Do not try to bring your whole home with you. Everything you need is easy to buy locally. Start with the minimum, and we will help you settle in.
