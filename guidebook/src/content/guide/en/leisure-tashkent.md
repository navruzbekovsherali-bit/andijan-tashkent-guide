Tashkent is the smell of fresh bread, shady plane trees, Soviet modernism and mirrored skyscrapers. Here are the places where you best feel the mood of the city.

## Sights

### The Hazrati Imam (Hast Imam) ensemble

Turquoise domes against a blue sky and one of the oldest Qurans in the world — the Uthman Quran. A place of calm and grandeur.

- **Address:** Zarqaynar Street (Old Town).

### Chorsu Bazaar

Not just a market but a real theatre of life. Under a huge blue dome people sell spices, dried apricots and nuts, and the air smells of grilled meat. Go up to the second tier — there is a great view from there.

- **Address:** 48 Navoi Street.

### The Tashkent metro

One of the most beautiful metros in the former Soviet Union: every station is a palace of its own. Look in at Kosmonavtlar (futuristic blue gloss) and Alisher Navoi (it looks like a mosque inside).

- **Website:** [tashmetro.uz](https://tashmetro.uz)

## Museums and beautiful places

### Museum of Applied Arts

The former mansion of a Russian diplomat with exquisite carving in ganch plaster and wood. Every hall is a work of art.

- **Address:** 15 Rakatboshi Street.

### Bonum Factum gallery

For lovers of contemporary art: exhibitions, film screenings and talks right in the city centre.

- **Address:** Sodiq Azimov Street, 3rd cul-de-sac.

### Palace of Grand Duke Nicholas Konstantinovich Romanov

An elegant Art Nouveau building of fired brick behind a wrought-iron fence. It is hard to get inside, but from the outside it is one of the most photogenic spots in the city.

- **Address:** Sharof Rashidov Avenue, near Mustaqillik Square.

## Restaurants and cafés

### National cuisine

**Blue Domes (Golubye Kupola).** A restaurant with history in a park: you can sit in the shade of huge trees. They serve shurpa and shashlik. The atmosphere of old Tashkent — unhurried and refined.

- **Address:** 77 Sharof Rashidov Street.

**Obi Hayot.** A restaurant-museum with wood carving, painting and national ornaments. Try the "khan's" plov or kazan kabob.

- **Address:** 19 Beshyog'och Street.

### Modern cuisine

**Socials Cafe.** A stylish interior, a young crowd and breakfast all day. Try the salmon Benedict or the signature desserts.

- **Address:** 36 Taras Shevchenko Street.

**Horiz.** A minimalist restaurant serving signature dishes from local produce in a modern style.

- **Address:** 44 Sodiq Azimov Street.

### Coffee shops

**B&B Coffee House.** One of the oldest coffee chains in the city. Locals love it for the blueberry cheesecake and cosy sofas.

- **Address:** 24 Shota Rustaveli Street (there are other branches too).

**Chaykof.** Fresh croissants, home-style lunches and warm conversation. Bright, cosy and European, but with Tashkent hospitality.

- **Address:** 16b Saida Baraka Street.

### Meat and grill

**The Meat.** A modern steakhouse for those who miss a good steak: generous portions and a good wine list.

- **Address:** 89 Nukus Street.

## Walks and relaxation

- **The Japanese Garden** — a quiet place with a pond, peacocks and neat paths.
- **Magic City** — a large amusement park with a castle, singing fountains and evening lights. Come after sunset.
- **Amir Temur Square** — the centre of the city. Sit on a bench under the centuries-old plane trees and watch city life go by.

Tashkent opens up gradually: choose your route, and the city will do the rest.
