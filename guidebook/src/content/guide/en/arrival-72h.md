This is the most important page for your first days. Follow the steps in order. They decide whether your phone will work and whether your stay in the country is legal.

## At the airport

<div class="video-reel" data-reel="arrival"></div>

1. **Declare your phone.** Go through the "red channel" and declare every device. At airports you can also do this online using a QR code. Keep all customs documents: you will need them to register the IMEI.
2. **Check all IMEI numbers.** If your phone takes two SIM cards, or a SIM and an eSIM, it has two IMEI numbers, and you must register each one. To find the IMEI, dial `*#06#`.
3. **Buy a SIM card.** Mobile operators (Beeline, Ucell, Mobiuz, Uztelecom) have desks right in the airport. A SIM card is issued only with your international passport. We recommend Ucell.
4. **Say that you are a non-resident** when you buy the SIM card.

> **Important.** Without a customs declaration you will have to pay 30% of the phone's value when you register the IMEI. From 01.01.2027 the rate drops to 20%.

## First 24 hours

1. **Contact your onboarding specialist.** Let them know you have arrived, and swap numbers so you can reach each other quickly.
2. **Give them your address.** Send your onboarding specialist the address of your hotel or flat.
3. **Turn on mobile data.** You need it to stay in touch and find your way around.
4. **Install apps** for taxis, maps and payments. The list and links are on the "Useful apps" page.
5. **Look around your neighbourhood:** shops, pharmacies, bus stops.

## First 48 hours

1. **Register at your place of stay.** This is your main legal step.
   - **In a hotel**, registration is done automatically. When you check out, ask for a printout of the registration slip.
   - **In a flat**, register yourself on [emehmon.uz](https://emehmon.uz), or go with the owner to the migration office.
2. **Get your documents ready:** international passport, the slip from your previous address (if you have one), and the owner's consent.
3. **Meet your Buddy.** Your mentor will help you settle into life at the bank faster.

## First 72 hours

1. **Check that your registration is done.** The deadline is 3 days after arrival. Every time you move to a new address, you register again, also within 3 days.
2. **Register your phone's IMEI** on [uzimei.uz](https://uzimei.uz) if you use a local SIM card. You can check the status by dialling `*1170#`, by sending your IMEI by SMS to 1170, on uzimei.uz or on my.gov.uz.
3. **Keep your documents:** customs declaration, registration slip, rental agreement.

> **Check with HR.** Sources give different deadlines for IMEI registration: 30 or 60 days from when you first connect a local SIM card. Check the exact deadline on uzimei.uz and do not put it off. If you miss it, your phone will be blocked on local networks. With a foreign SIM card in roaming, you do not need to register.

## Registration deadlines and fines

| Who | Registration deadline |
|---|---|
| Citizens of Russia | not needed for stays of up to 15 days |
| All other foreign citizens | within 3 days |
| When you move to a new address | within 3 days |

> **Check with HR.** We have not yet confirmed whether the new registration law (ZRU-1074, in force since October 2025) means working days or calendar days. To be safe, count calendar days.

| Days late | Fine |
|---|---|
| up to 10 days | 5 BCA — 2.2 million soums |
| 10 to 30 days | 10 BCA — 4.4 million soums |
| more than 30 days | 20 BCA — 8.8 million soums |

The amounts are based on a BCA (base calculation amount, БРВ) of 440,000 soums (from 01.09.2026). If you do not pay the fine, you may be deported and banned from entering the country for 1 year, or for 3 years for a repeat offence.

> **Check with HR.** A draft law to make the fines stricter is still being revised. Ask your onboarding specialist which version currently applies.

If you have done all these steps, well done. This is one of the most important stages of settling in, and it gets easier from here. If anything is unclear, do not hesitate to ask your onboarding specialist. Even simple questions are perfectly fine.
