## Working schedule

The bank works a five-day week, 40 hours a week.

| | Time |
|---|---|
| Start of the day | 08:30 |
| Lunch | 12:30–13:30 |
| End of the day | 17:30 |
| Weekends | Saturday and Sunday |

If your schedule differs from the standard one, check the details with HR.

## Public holidays and days off in 2026

On these days the whole country officially has a day off. Plan trips and time with family and friends in advance.

| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| 1 January | New Year |
| 2 January | Extra day off |
| 8 March | International Women's Day |
| 20 March | Ramadan Hayit (Eid al-Fitr) |
| 21 March | Navruz |
| 9 May | Day of Remembrance and Honour |
| 27 May | Qurban Hayit (Eid al-Adha) |
| 28–29 May | Extra days off |
| 31 August | Extra day off |
| 1 September | Independence Day |
| 1 October | Teachers' and Mentors' Day |
| 8 December | Constitution Day |

The Hayit dates and the extra days off were set by Presidential Decree UP-257 of 24.12.2025.

> **Check with HR.** If a holiday falls on a weekend, the day off is moved. As of October 2026, 9 March, 23 March and 11 May were also days off in 2026. Check the moved days against the decree or ask HR.

## Annual leave

- **How many days.** Under the Labour Code, the minimum annual leave is **21 calendar days** (Art. 216). At our bank it is **28 calendar days**.
- **Public holidays** are not counted as leave days.
- **Extra leave for length of service** (Art. 220): 2 days for every 5 years of work, but no more than 8 days.
- **Your first leave** can be taken **after 6 months** of work (Art. 227). Earlier by agreement with the employer.

> **Tip.** Agree your leave dates with your manager in advance. That way the team has time to share out your tasks, and you can rest without work calls.

Articles refer to the new Labour Code (in force since 30.04.2023).
