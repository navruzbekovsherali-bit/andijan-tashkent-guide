Andijan is a city where ancient minarets stand next to modern avenues and the smell of fresh flatbread follows you everywhere. Nobody rushes here: this is a city to be tasted.

## Sights

### The Jami complex

The architectural gem of the city: a 19th-century mosque and madrasah with fine wood carving.

- **Where:** Old Town, Lutfiy Street.

### Babur Park

Andijan is the birthplace of Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur. The memorial park on Bogishamol Hill holds a symbolic tomb of the poet and ruler. From here you can see the whole city.

- **Where:** the south-east of the city, Bogishamol Hill.

### The Old Town (Eski Shahar)

Narrow streets, old houses and the atmosphere of an eastern mahalla — the real Andijan.

- **Where:** the area around the Yangi Bazaar market.

## Museums and beautiful places

### State Museum of Literature and Art

Unique manuscripts and paintings. The building itself looks like a treasure chest.

- **Where:** Amir Temur Street.

### Andijan Regional Museum of Local History

From the bones of ancient animals to fine ceramics: here you can see how the city changed over the centuries.

- **Where:** Babur Avenue.

### Old Town craft workshops

Drop in on hereditary wood carvers and potters — their work is fascinating to watch and beautiful to photograph.

- **Where:** the streets around the Jami complex.

## Restaurants and cafés

Andijan is considered one of the food capitals of Uzbekistan.

### National cuisine

**Uzbegim teahouse.** Try the Andijan plov and dimlama.

- **Address:** Navoi Street.

**Osh Markazi (Plov Centre).** Local plov made with devzira rice — quick and authentic.

- **Address:** the central market area.

### Modern cuisine

**Zaytun.** A stylish place with European-level service and a varied menu — good for a nice dinner.

- **Address:** Milliy Tiklanish Street.

### Cafés

**Coffee Time.** Lattes and desserts — a good place to rest between walks.

- **Address:** Babur Avenue.

## Walks and relaxation

- **Navoi Square.** In the evening the lights come on, families stroll and children ride scooters.
- **Eco Park.** Lots of greenery, walking paths and fresh air — a good end to a busy day.
