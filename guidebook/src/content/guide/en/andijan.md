Andijan is a city where ancient traditions sit alongside industry and modern avenues.

## Cultural Capital of the Turkic World

Andijan is more than 2500 years old. In **2026** it holds the title of **Cultural Capital of the Turkic World**, and the city hosts international festivals and forums. Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur was born here: a poet, a ruler and the founder of the Mughal Empire.

### Facts and figures

- **Population.** About 500,000 people in the city and **3.53 million** in the region (as of 01.04.2026). Andijan Region is the most densely populated region in the country.
- **Economy.** Andijan is an industrial centre (engineering and textiles), surrounded by orchards and vineyards.

## Climate

If you find the Tashkent heat hard to bear, you will be more comfortable here: the mountains soften the winter, and the summer is fresher.

- **April–May:** the foothills are covered in tulips.
- **September–October:** harvest season.

> **Tip.** Be sure to try Andijan flatbread ("Andijon noni") and black mulberry juice. The best gifts from here are knives and silk.

## Transport

From **01.01.2026** public transport in Andijan is cashless.

| How you pay | Price |
|---|---|
| ATTO card or bank card | 2100 soums |
| Single QR ticket (cash) | 3000 soums |

**How to get here from Tashkent:**

1. **By train** through the scenic tunnel on the Kamchik Pass. From 21.10.2026 a new electric train No. 732/731 runs on the route. Tickets are on [eticket.railway.uz](https://eticket.railway.uz).
2. **By plane** to Andijan airport.

**Around the city**, it is easy to get about by taxi with Yandex Go or by "Damas" minibuses (marshrutka).

## Where to go

1. **Jami Complex:** the heart of the old city. The complex survived the devastating earthquake of 1902. Look out for the wood carving.
2. **Bogishamol Hill:** a green area with clean air and panoramic views of the city.
3. **Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur House Museum:** a museum about the life of the great ruler and poet.
4. **Shahrikhan craft centres:** a short trip out of town. Here you can see how the famous Andijan knives (pichoq) are made and buy a souvenir from a craftsman.

More places, restaurants and cafés are on the "Leisure in Andijan" page.
