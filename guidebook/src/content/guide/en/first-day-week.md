We have prepared everything for a comfortable start. Below is the plan for your first day and first week.

## Before your first day

- **Time.** The working day is **08:30 to 17:30**. On your first day, arrive by 08:30.
- **Who will meet you.** The HR onboarding specialist or your Buddy.
- **How to get there and where to park.** Check with your onboarding specialist.
- **Contact phone number.** Check with your onboarding specialist.
- **Dress code.** Check with your onboarding specialist.

### What to bring

- **Essential:** your original passport and the documents for your paperwork, as listed by HR.
- **Useful:** your own laptop (if you have one), a notebook, a pen and a bottle of water.

## Your first day

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Arrive at the office |
| 09:00–10:00 | Meet your onboarding specialist and Buddy, office tour, receive your pass |
| 10:00–12:00 | Paperwork: employment contract, forms, setting up your workplace |
| 12:30–13:30 | Lunch. Your Buddy will show you where to eat and introduce you to colleagues |
| 14:00–16:00 | Meeting with your manager: overview of tasks and projects, access to systems |
| 16:00–17:00 | Questions and answers, discussing your onboarding plan |

> **Tip.** Do not worry: the first day is for getting to know people. Nobody expects you to work at full speed straight away.

**PINFL.** HR will help you get your PINFL in your first days. We help with the paperwork from the moment you arrive.

## Your first week

The first week is when you move from getting to know people to working actively in the team.

### Day 2. Tools and access

- Check all your company access: email and internal systems.
- Kick-off meeting with your manager: KPIs and goals for your probation period.

### Day 3. Getting into the processes

- Learn the bank's internal structure and key regulations.
- Lunch with the team: an informal get-together.

### Day 4. Documents

- A visit to government offices to get your PINFL (with HR support).
- Check the IMEI registration status of your phone.

### Day 5. Wrapping up the week

- One-to-one with your Buddy or HR: how did your first week go?
- Review of your tasks and a plan for the next week.

## Checklist for the end of the week

- [ ] Your workplace is set up and you have all the access you need.
- [ ] You understand your key tasks for the coming month.
- [ ] Your registration is done and your document applications are under way.
- [ ] You have met the key colleagues in your department.

## A word from your onboarding specialist

> Hello! My name is Sherali, and I am an onboarding specialist. My job is to help you settle in faster, understand all the processes and feel confident from your first days. Do not hesitate to ask questions, even if they seem simple. That is what I am here for.
>
> A small piece of advice: do not try to take in everything at once. Settling in is a journey, not an exam. Go step by step, write down what matters, and remember: there are people around you who truly want you to succeed. We will get through it together.
