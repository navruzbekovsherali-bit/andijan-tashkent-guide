This page covers the payment schedule, how your income is made up and taxes.

## When the money arrives

<div class="video-reel" data-reel="salary"></div>

Salary is paid **3 times a month**:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| by the 12th | advance |
| by the 25th | KPI |
| on the 31st | salary |

In addition, financial assistance for household goods is paid on the 1st.

## What your income is made of

- **Base salary** — the fixed part.
- **Bonuses** — depend on your individual KPIs, project results or your unit's performance.

## Taxes and currency

- Payments are made in the national currency — **soums** — to a **HUMO** card.
- **Personal income tax** is a flat **12%**.
