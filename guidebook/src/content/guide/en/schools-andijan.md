All of them are in the city of Andijan.

## How to choose a kindergarten

1. **Age.** Private kindergartens usually accept children from 1.5–2 years old.
2. **Settling in.** Ask about trial days and the settling-in period.
3. **Routine.** Check the daily schedule, meals and the language spoken in the groups.
4. **Group size.** In small groups it is easier for a child to adjust after a move.

## Documents

You usually need:

- a parent's passport;
- the child's birth certificate;
- the child's medical record.

Check the exact list with the school or kindergarten you choose.
