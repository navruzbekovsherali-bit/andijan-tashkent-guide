You can get an income certificate in two ways: online on government portals or through the bank's Accounting department.

## Option 1. Online on government portals

Best if you need the certificate urgently. It is generated immediately.

1. **Register with OneID** (id.egov.uz), the single sign-in system for government services. You will need your PINFL for it.
2. **Log in to one of these services:**
   - **my3.soliq.uz:** the taxpayer's personal account, "Personal information" section (Персональная информация);
   - **[my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz):** the single government services portal. In the search, type "справка о доходах" (income certificate).
3. **Submit the request** and download the finished certificate.

## Option 2. Through the EDMS

Best for official document flow through the bank's Accounting department. **It takes 15 to 30 days.**

1. Create a letter in the EDMS (ЭДО) and include:
   - **Full name**, written out in full;
   - **TIN** (ИНН): your taxpayer identification number;
   - **Period:** the start month and end month the certificate should cover.
2. Send the letter to **Accounting**.
3. Be sure to copy in the **bank's tax specialists**.

Once you send it, the process starts automatically.
