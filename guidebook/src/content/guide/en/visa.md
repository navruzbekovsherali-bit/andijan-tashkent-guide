The bank is inviting you to work and acts as your inviting party, so it does most of the paperwork. What it needs from you is a carefully completed form and a visit to the consulate.

## Step by step

1. **Fill in the visa application form** on [visa.mfa.uz](https://visa.mfa.uz). Write your name, surname and passport number in Latin letters, exactly as in your passport.
2. **Upload a 3×4 photo and your passport.** The photo should be recent and in colour; the passport page with your photo, without glare. Then **check that every detail is correct and up to date**: the passport’s expiry date, its number, the dates and the spelling of your name.
3. **Give the finished form to the adaptation specialist.**
4. **The bank files the documents with the Foreign Ministry.** The recruitment and adaptation team prepares an official letter to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Uzbekistan and sends the package of documents on behalf of the bank as the inviting party.
5. **Wait for the answer.** 10–15 working days after filing, you hear whether the visa is ready. The adaptation specialist will let you know.
6. **Collect the visa at the consulate** of Uzbekistan in your own country.

> **Important.** **The visa is paid for at the consulate, by the employee. The bank does not pay or refund the consular fee.**

## Check before you send

- Your passport is valid for the whole trip, with time to spare. If it expires soon, renew it first.
- Name and surname in Latin letters, exactly as in the passport.
- Passport number, issue and expiry dates match the passport.
- The 3×4 photo is recent and sharp.
- The whole passport page is readable on the scan.
- The phone number and email in the form work: notifications go there.

## Who does what

| Stage | Who |
|---|---|
| Form, 3×4 photo, passport | you |
| Check and hand over the form | you → adaptation specialist |
| Letter and documents to the Foreign Ministry | recruitment and adaptation team |
| Answer on the visa — 10–15 working days | Foreign Ministry |
| Collecting the visa and paying the fee | you, at the consulate |

## Once you have the visa

1. **Tell the adaptation specialist** that you have the visa and agree on your arrival date.
2. **Pack** — the list is on the page [“What to pack”](../packing/).
3. **After you land** you have 3 days to register where you live — see [“Registration: the 3-day rule”](../registration/).

> **Tip.** Take photos of your visa and passport and keep them on your phone and in the cloud. If a document gets lost, a copy makes replacing it much easier.
