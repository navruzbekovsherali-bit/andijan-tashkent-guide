Below are the basic rules of Uzbekistan's medical system and tips on how to get help.

## Emergency care

- **Ambulance: 103.** Works across the whole country. Operators speak Uzbek and Russian.
- **Private ambulance.** Some large private clinics have their own ambulances, and you can call them directly.

> **Important.** Foreigners receive only urgent (emergency) care free of charge, when there is a threat to life or health. Further treatment is paid.

## Rules for foreigners

Under the official rules, foreign citizens:

1. receive **urgent care free of charge**;
2. **pay for further treatment themselves**;
3. can be treated **under a voluntary health insurance policy (ДМС)**.

## Clinics

- **State hospitals** provide care, but equipment and service may not meet international standards, especially outside the large cities.
- **Private clinics** are the more common choice for foreigners, for diagnostics, planned treatment and operations. They usually have more modern equipment, quicker appointments and English-speaking staff.
- **Tashkent** has the best-developed medical care, including international clinics.

## Language

Russian is widely spoken in Uzbekistan, and most doctors understand it. If you do not speak Russian, find someone in advance who can help translate.

## Pharmacies

- Large pharmacy chains are in the centre of every city.
- Most medicines are easy to find.
- **Antibiotics** have been sold only with an electronic prescription since 15.12.2025. If you take any medicines regularly, bring a supply with you.

## Health insurance

Planned treatment, diagnostics and private clinics are usually paid, so insurance is highly advisable. Choose a policy that covers your stay abroad, emergency care and, if needed, medical evacuation.

You can get insurance:

1. **before you travel**, from an international insurance company;
2. **locally**, from a local insurance company or through an insurance broker who will help you choose a voluntary health insurance (ДМС) policy.

> **Tip.** Before buying a policy, check your benefits package at the bank and compare terms and prices on insurance companies' websites.
