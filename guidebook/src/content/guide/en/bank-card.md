## How to open a salary card

Your onboarding specialist will help you open a Hamkorbank card for receiving your salary. The whole process takes **1–2 days**. All you need are your documents and one visit to the bank.

### 1. Prepare your documents

- passport (**original**);
- registration at your place of stay;
- **PINFL**;
- a completed form and a signed application.

> **Important.** Before you send the full set of documents, fill in the form, sign it, scan it and send it to your onboarding specialist.

### 2. Visit a bank branch

1. Your onboarding specialist will book an appointment at a time that suits you.
2. At the branch, sign the documents. The bank employee speaks **English and Russian**.
3. The visit takes **10–15 minutes**.

Ask your onboarding specialist for the branch address.

### 3. Set up mobile banking

1. Download the Hamkorbank app. The links are on the [mobile banking page](https://hamkorbank.uz/en/personal-banking/mobile-banking).
2. Sign up using your card.
3. Set a **PIN code** and fingerprint login.

Available card types: Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, HUMO and Uzcard. Ask your onboarding specialist about limits, fees and the details of the corporate account.

## Exchanging currency

### Where to exchange

- **Official exchange desks** are in every branch of Hamkorbank and other banks.
- **Take your original passport.** Without it, they will not exchange your money.
- **The rate** is close to the market rate, and the fee is 0–1%.
- **Currency:** dollars and euros are the most widely used. Roubles are accepted, but the rate is usually less favourable.
- **Check the current rate** in the Hamkorbank app: it is often better there than at the cash desk.

### Where not to exchange

- **With street money changers.** It is illegal and unsafe.
- **At airports and with taxi drivers.** You will lose on the exchange rate.

## Card or cash

**By card** (Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay) it is easy to pay in supermarkets (Korzinka, Makro), shopping centres, hotels, restaurants in the centre and at chain petrol stations. Link your card to apps such as Yandex Go, so you do not have to take it out every time.

**Cash** is useful at bazaars (Chorsu and others), in small local shops, for street food and for tips. In a taxi ordered through an app you can pay by card, but if you hail a car on the street, it is cash only.

> **Tip.** Always keep 50,000–100,000 soums in cash in your wallet for small expenses.

## ATMs

- ATMs are everywhere and work 24 hours a day.
- The withdrawal fee is usually 1–2%.
- You can withdraw the equivalent of about $500–1000 at a time.

> **Tip.** ATMs inside bank branches are more reliable and less likely to run out of cash by the evening.
