Where to shop in Uzbekistan depends on what you need: quick and comfortable, fresh and colourful, or delivered to your home.

## Supermarkets

Good when you need to buy everything in one place.

| Chain | Why it is convenient | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Korzinka | The largest chain, a wide range — from fresh bread to exotic fruit | A handy mobile app and cashback |
| Makro | For big shopping trips, often in hypermarket format | Get the free card — prices are lower with it |
| Havas | A "shop near home", affordable prices, many local brands | Handy for everyday shopping: milk, bread |

## Bazaars

Food here is especially tasty, and shopping turns into a small adventure.

- **Chorsu and Ippodrom** in Tashkent are the best-known bazaars. You can find everything here, from flatbread to traditional robes.
- **Bargain.** Offering your own price is normal; it shows you are interested.
- **Freshness.** Meat, vegetables and fruit come to the stalls straight from orchards and farms.
- **Taste.** At a bazaar sellers usually let you try things — it makes it easier to pick the sweetest watermelon or the softest kurt.

## Online delivery

1. **Uzum Market** — the largest marketplace, from electronics to cosmetics. Delivery is usually next day, by courier or to a pickup point.
2. **Uzum Tezkor** — fast delivery from restaurants and shops, usually in 30–60 minutes.
3. **Yandex Eats and Lavka** — groceries and ready meals, work well in big cities.

Links to the apps are on the "Useful apps" page.

> **Tip.** Choose local produce: vegetables and fruit grown here are cheaper than imported ones and much tastier. Try the local tomatoes — you will taste the difference straight away.
