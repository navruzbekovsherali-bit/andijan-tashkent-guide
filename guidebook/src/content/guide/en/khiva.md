Khiva is an open-air museum behind massive clay walls. Inside the old town there are no cars and no noise — just endless sun and turquoise majolica against sand-coloured walls. Itchan Kala is a fortress city where people still live.

## How to get there

Khiva is in the west of Uzbekistan, in the Khorezm oasis, near the Kyzylkum Desert.

1. **By plane** — the fastest way. The nearest airport is in **Urgench**, 30 km from Khiva. There are flights from Tashkent and some foreign cities. From Urgench to Khiva takes 20–30 minutes by taxi.
2. **The Jaloliddin Manguberdi high-speed train** — since **5 May 2026** it runs from Tashkent to Khiva, taking about **7.5 h**.
3. **Night train** — fall asleep in the capital, wake up at the gates of the ancient city.
4. **The Urgench–Khiva trolleybus** — a slow but cheap and colourful intercity route.

Train tickets are on the official website [eticket.railway.uz](https://eticket.railway.uz).

## What to see in Itchan Kala

Itchan Kala is the inner city behind the fortress wall. You buy one ticket at the entrance and walk around the museums.

> **Check on the spot.** As of October 2026, entrance tickets are sold at the ticket office by the West Gate (Ota Darvoza). Online sales have not been confirmed.

### The Kalta Minor minaret

The "unfinished" minaret in bright blue tiles. It was meant to be the tallest in the world but stayed short — and became the symbol of Khiva.

### Juma Mosque

There is no gold or bright colour inside, but there are 213 carved wooden columns. Each has its own pattern, and some are more than a thousand years old.

### Kunya-Ark

The old fortress of the rulers of Khiva. Climb the Ak-Sheikh-Bobo viewpoint at sunset — it has the best view over the city's rooftops.

### Pahlavan Mahmud Mausoleum

The most beautiful courtyard: the walls are covered from floor to ceiling with the finest blue and white painting. It is cool and peaceful here.

## What to try

Khorezm cuisine is very different from Samarkand cuisine.

- **Shivit oshi** — bright green dill noodles served with meat and a sour cream sauce.
- **Tukhum barak** — "dumplings" filled with egg. The recipe is passed down from generation to generation.
- **Terrassa restaurant** — opposite the Kalta Minor minaret, a good place for dinner with a view of the illuminated city.

## Useful tips

- **Walk early in the morning or in the evening.** It is very hot during the day and the stones heat up a lot. At dawn there are almost no tourists, and the photos come out better.
- **Souvenirs.** Khiva makes fluffy sheepskin hats called chugurma. Even if you do not buy one, try it on for a photo.
- **A walk along the wall.** You can walk along the city wall. Climb up near the north gate and walk the perimeter — the whole ancient city will be laid out before you.
