Below are the main ways to get around the city, their advantages and some useful tips.

## Taxis

The most convenient and popular option.

- **Apps:** Yandex Go is the main service across the country. In Tashkent there is also MyTaxi.
- **Always order through an app.** It is safer and the price is fairer.
- **Price:** check the exact fare in the app before you order.

> **Tip.** Taxi prices go up at rush hour and when it rains. In Tashkent it is easier to take the metro at those times.

## Metro (Tashkent only)

The Tashkent metro is not just transport but a real underground museum.

- **Fare (from 01.10.2026):** 2500 soums by card or app, 3000 soums in cash.
- **How to pay:** buy a reusable Atto transport card at the ticket office. It saves time.
- **Hours:** roughly 05:00 to 00:00.
- **Conditions:** clean and safe, and in summer the air conditioning is a lifesaver.

> **Check with HR.** We could not find an official metro timetable. Check the times of the first and last trains at the station.

## Buses

Good if you are not in a hurry and want to feel the rhythm of the city.

### In Tashkent

- **Fare (from 01.10.2026):** 2500 soums by card, 3000 soums in cash.
- **Payment:** Atto card or cash.

### In Andijan

- **From 01.01.2026 transport is cashless:** a ride costs 2100 soums by card.
- **With cash** you can only buy a single QR ticket for 3000 soums.

> **Tip.** Install 2GIS: the app shows routes and where your bus is right now. Buses are crowded at rush hour, so allow extra time.

## Car hire

Good if you want complete freedom to get around.

- **Documents:** an international driving permit is required.
- **Price:** according to the onboarding team, from $30 to $50 a day.
- **Where to hire:** there are international chains (for example, Europcar) and local rental companies.
- **Traffic** drives on the right.

> **Important.** The local driving style is different from Europe: drivers often use the horn and do not always use their indicators. Stay alert.

## On foot

The best way to see the city, with a few caveats.

- **Routes** are easy to plan in 2GIS, so you will not get lost in the mahallas.
- **Pavements** are not always even, so wear comfortable shoes.
- **In summer** it is very hot during the day. Plan walks for the morning or evening.

## Getting to work

For daily trips we recommend a taxi, or the metro if your home and the office are near stations. It is the most reliable way not to be late for an important meeting.
