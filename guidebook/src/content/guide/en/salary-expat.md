This page covers how you are paid, taxes, and practical tips on currency and cash.

## When the money arrives

<div class="video-reel" data-reel="salary"></div>

Salary is paid **once a month**, usually **by the 10th**.

## What your income is made of

- **Base salary:** the fixed part. The amount is in your offer.
- **Bonuses:** depend on your individual KPIs, project results or your unit's performance. The terms are in your offer.

## Taxes and currency

- Payments inside the country are made in **soums** to a **HUMO** or **Uzcard** card.
- **Personal income tax** (НДФЛ) is a flat **12%**, the same for residents and non-residents. Non-residents get no tax reliefs.
- You are a tax resident if you are in Uzbekistan for 183 days or more in any 12 consecutive months.
- If the amount in your offer is **net**, the bank covers the 12% tax.

## Transfers to a Visa card and a foreign account

- You can move money to a **Hamkorbank Visa** card on the same day with no loss on the exchange rate.
- If you gave the details of an account in another country when your offer was prepared, your salary will be transferred to that account.

> **Check with HR.** The guidebook says that payments in USD to accounts outside Uzbekistan (for up to 6 months) are possible within current law. As of October 2026 this condition needs confirmation: check it with HR or the bank's currency control service.

## Exchanging currency

- Exchange money only at official exchange desks in banks (you need your **passport**) or in banking apps.
- The rate is close to the market rate, and the fee is **0–1%**.
- The US dollar is the most widely used currency; euros are also exchanged everywhere. The rouble rate is usually less favourable.

## Withdrawing cash

- ATMs accept Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay cards.
- The withdrawal fee is usually **1–2%**, and the limit is about **$500–1000** per transaction.

See the current exchange rate on [hamkorbank.uz](https://hamkorbank.uz).
