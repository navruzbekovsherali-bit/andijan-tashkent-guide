Moving to a new country always means a little excitement and a lot of new things. Uzbekistan is a hospitable country with a rich history, and we will help you feel at home here.

## A country of opportunity

Modern Uzbekistan combines the heritage of the Great Silk Road with active reforms.

- **Population.** The country has **39.5 million people** (as of 1 October 2026).
- **Many nationalities.** Society is multinational, and hospitality here is a way of life.
- **Languages.** The state language is **Uzbek**. **Russian** is widely used in business and in everyday life in cities.

> **Tip.** Learn a couple of basic phrases: "Assalomu alaykum" (hello) and "Rahmat" (thank you). Locals really appreciate it. More phrases are on the "Speak like a local" page.

## Safety

Uzbekistan is one of the safest countries in the region.

- **Ranking.** 25th place in the Numbeo world safety index (2025).
- **Crime.** The crime index is **26.6**, which is low.

## Good to know

### Teahouse culture

Many matters here are settled over a cup of tea. If a colleague invites you "for tea", do not say no: it is the best way to quickly become part of the team.

### Sun

There are more than 300 sunny days a year here. Good sunglasses are not an accessory but a necessity.
